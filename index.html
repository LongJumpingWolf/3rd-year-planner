        Data audit

        What is verified, estimated, or still waiting for a title-level import.

        Data integrity rules

        • Missing durations are estimated at 20 minutes and clearly flagged.
        • Potential duplicates stay separate until you confirm them.
        • FMT (44 lectures, 34 h 36 min as listed) and ENT (65 lectures, 47 h 23 min) are in the library with every title and duration. The FMT list adds up to 40 minutes more than the 33 h 56 min total recorded earlier, so one FMT duration may be off; edit it in the Task library if you spot it.
        • PSM entries 49 and 50 are a possible duplicate and remain separate.
        • Posting catch-up uses a provisional 3 hours per missed weekday; delete those blocks if your real missed sessions differ.
        • The official timetable and your reported vacation dates differ; the scheduler uses your 1–13 Nov break.

        CSV columns: title, subject, category, durationSec (required); id, sourceDuration, status, type (optional). Export first to get a matching sample.

        Settings

        These assumptions drive every time estimate in the app.

        Study-time assumptions

        Daily study blocks

        Two separate capacities. Light work fits around college and postings; focused work is for lectures and difficult concepts at home. They are never pooled into one bucket.

        Home by about 5:45 PM, rested by 6:30 PM.
        Focused work is planned for the hours you set; any day that would run past this time (minus 15 minutes) is flagged.
        Weekday planning capacity = focused + college-gap hours. Re-run the scheduler afterwards to apply it to the calendar.

        Academic constraints currently recorded

        • College and postings: weekdays and Saturday, with clinical posting blocks in the morning.
        • Weekend DAMS: offline classes 9 AM–9 PM starting 13 November 2026.
        • Trip protection: about 1–15 December 2026; missed teaching becomes a separate catch-up category.
        • Post-end preparation: protect the week before each post-end examination.
        • Daily routine: home around 5:45 PM, rested by about 6:30 PM, bedtime by 10:30 PM. Focused work is scheduled from 6:30 PM with breaks and finishes before bedtime where possible.

        Cloud sync

        Viewer link

        Share a simple, big-print page with a parent or friend. They see overall progress, on track or behind, how much of today's work is done, a streak, this week, lectures per subject and your "Also done" notes. Lecture titles appear only for lectures you are behind on, so they can remind you. They never see your planner or any controls. Anyone who has the link can open it, so share it only with people you trust.
