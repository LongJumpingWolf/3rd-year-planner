Data integrity rules

  • Missing durations are estimated at 20 minutes and explicitly flagged.
  • Potential duplicates remain separate until confirmed.
  • FMT (44 videos / 33 h 56 min) and ENT (65 / 47 h 23 min) totals are preserved, but their title-level entries are not present in the current embedded data. They are not silently fabricated or treated as individual videos.
  • PSM duplicate entries 49 and 50 remain separate.
  • Clinical posting catch-up uses a provisional 3 hours per missed posting day; edit or delete these generated tasks if your actual missed sessions differ.
  • The official timetable and user-reported vacation dates differ; the scheduler uses the user-reported 1–13 Nov break assumption.
